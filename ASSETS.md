# Imágenes del catálogo

Generadas y editadas con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Cada archivo contiene dos vistas conceptuales del mismo diseño, frontal y superior; la galería presenta ambas por separado. Archivos finales en `site/<id>.webp`.

## bloom

Archivo: `site/bloom.webp`

Prompt final:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: ecommerce two-photo diptych. Create exactly TWO equally sized SQUARE product photographs SIDE BY SIDE in one 2:1 landscape image, NO borders, NO dividing gutter, NO captions. LEFT HALF: a complete pair of foam clogs in three-quarter front view, entire shoes in frame. RIGHT HALF: the SAME EXACT complete pair and SAME charms photographed from directly above, openings clearly visible. Keep identical colors and charm designs in both photographs. Premium photorealistic studio footwear catalog, softly lit pale blush seamless backgrounds, beautiful soft shadows. Each photo independently composed within its half with comfortable margins. Physically detailed wearable decorative charms attached to the ventilation holes on top of EACH clog, not floating outside shoes. No watermarks, no branding except explicitly requested small nameplate charms. This is a fictional PaulitaM concept catalog. Product brief: Pastel pink spring clogs decorated with sculptural daisy, butterfly and tulip charms.
